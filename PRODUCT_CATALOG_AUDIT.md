# ESSCOM Product Catalog Audit

## Scope and counting method

This catalog contains **113 distinct profile-sourced product, equipment-family, or service references** in **13 categories**. Repeated model/specification mentions are consolidated where a family entry is clearer; named model variants with source-stated information remain separate records. A service and historical project-equipment reference are included and labeled. This count is not a claim of current inventory or ESSCOM ownership.

The supplied profile is a Techno World International group document. Only the ESS-Queue Management System and the display marked “Powered by ESSCOM Solutions” are specifically identified as ESSCom Solutions references. Items explicitly labeled TECHNOVATION, Techno Solar, or Wallers are kept under their named section. Other group-profile records are shown as ownership unconfirmed.

## Known limits and missing facts

- Current product availability, pricing, warranties, manufacturer relationships, compatibility, and current model identity are not consistently supplied by this historical profile.
- Many broad product references occur in the group introduction and do not have a specific division or model attached.
- Legacy specification text may describe old models and should not be used to select current equipment without a current datasheet.
- Profile page numbers are approximate locators; DOCX pagination differs by Word version. See XML section references in MEDIA_INVENTORY.md.
- No ESSCOM phone, WhatsApp, email, address, official domain, or social details were taken from the Salman Traders closing page.
- Image generation was not necessary because profile category photos or existing illustrative fallbacks were available.

## Root cause of the queue-only catalog

src/data/products.ts previously contained an empty products array. With no published products, /products fell back to equipmentCatalog, which was derived solely from publishedSolutions. Only queue-management was published, so every other category was filtered out. The catalog also contained no source-profile records. This implementation centralizes wider profile references and separate category attribution metadata; the Solutions directory remains queue-only because other ESSCOM services are not confirmed.

## Technical implementation

- Centralized records in src/data/products.ts and category metadata in src/data/product-categories.ts.
- Search, category filtering, sorting, result count, and clear filters use the same records.
- Product detail routes are generated statically for every profile-reference record.
- Cards/details identify category, division attribution, source pages, and historical/reference status.
- Specifications appear only where text occurs in the profile and are labeled as historical source details.
- Quote actions remain absent while no verified contact channel is configured.
